## Contributions

This is a **sample monthly report**. Replace the prompts with actual work; there are no contribution totals or achievements to infer from this page.

For each contribution, link the repository and pull request, summarize the problem, and state its current status: draft, in review, merged, or closed.

## Work in progress

Unfinished work belongs here too. Write down what you tried, where you are stuck, and what would make the next attempt useful.

## Reading and observations

Link the discussions, specifications, or code you read. A sentence about why each mattered is enough.

## Next month

Choose a few concrete next steps. Keep them separate from completed work so that the record stays honest.
